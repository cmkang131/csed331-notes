Q1
(20 points) Let G be a directed graph, and s and t be two vertices of G. Prove the max-flow min-cut theorem: The size of the maximum s-t flow in G equals the capacity of the smallest (s, t)-cut of G.
Q2
(20 points) Given an undirected graph G = (V, E), we want to find a spanning tree of G in which each non-leaf node has exactly two children, if it exists. Clearly, this problem is in NP. Then show that this problem is NP-complete.
Q3
(20 points) Give a polynomial-time 2-approximation algorithm for the vertex cover problem. Then analyze your algorithm.
Q4
(20 points) Let G = (V, E) be an undirected simple graph. Design an efficient algorithm for finding a maximum-cardinality subset U of V such that for every vertex u of U , there are at least five vertices of U adjacent to u, and there are at least five vertices of U which are not adjacent to U . Then analyze the correctness and time complexity of your algorithm.
주석: 'not adjacent to U'는 인쇄 원문 그대로 [sic] — 'u'의 오타로 읽는 해석과 분리해 둔다.
Q5
(20 points) Let T1 and T2 be two trees with specified roots, say v1 and v2 . A connected subgraph H of T1 is isomorphic to T2 if there is an injective function f : V (H) → V (T2 ) with f (vH ) = v2 such that u and v are adjacent in H iff f (u) and f (v) are adjacent in T2 for any two vertices u and v of H, where vH denote the vertex of H with smallest depth, and V (H) and V (T2 ) are the vertex sets of H and T2 , respectively. Design an efficient algorithm for checking if there is a connected subgraph of T1 isomorphic to T2 . Then analyze the running time of your algorithm.
주석: isomorphic 정의는 원문 그대로다. H의 선택이 문자 그대로면 자명하다는 해석 문제는 원문과 분리해 표시한다.
