Q1
(20pts) Give a sorting algorithm whose average time complexity is O(n log n), where n is the number of input values. Show that the average time complexity is O(n log n) for that algorithm.
Q2
(20pts) Let G be a weighted undirected graph that contains no cycle. Let n be the number of vertices in G. The time complexities in (b) and (c) below should be represented in terms of n. (a) Give an efficient algorithm that computes the longest path length between two given vertices of G. (b) If the edges of G are represented using adjacency lists, what is the time complexity of the algorithm you have proposed in (a)? (c) If the edges of G are represented using an adjacency matrix, what is the time complexity of the algorithm you have proposed in (a)?
Q3
(20pts) Professor Tiger claims that “any edge of minimum weight in an undirected connected graph G must be part of some MST of G.” Either prove the claim if it is correct, or give a counterexample if it is not correct.
Q4
(20pts) Let P be a convex polygon in the plane whose n vertices p1 , p2 , . . . , pn are given (stored in an array) in clockwise order along its boundary satisfying that p1 is the leftmost vertex, pn is the rightmost vertex pn , and pi lies above the line segment p1 pn for all 1 < i < n . Given a line `, we would like to find the vertex of P where a translated copy of ` is tangent to P from above. Design a O(log n)-time algorithm for finding such a tangent vertex, and analyze the running time.
Q5
(20pts) Let G = (V, E) be an undirected graph. In the Independent Set problem we are given an integer g, and the aim is to find a set D ⊆ V of g vertices such that no two vertices of D have an edge between them. In the Vertex Cover problem, we are given an integer b, and the aim is to find a C ⊆ V of b vertices such that every edge of E is incident to at least one vertex of C. Show that Vertex Cover is NP-complete, assuming that Independent Set is NP-complete.
