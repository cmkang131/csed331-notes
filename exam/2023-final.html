문제 본문은 원문 영어 그대로 옮긴 것이다. 모호한 스캔·오타는 고치지 않고 주석으로 표시했다. 출처: Drive 즐겨찾기 '4분반 / 알고리즘(오은진) / 기출' PDF와 SHA-256 대조 완료.

Q1
(10 points) Given two strings x = x1 x2 . . . xn and y = y1 y2 . . . ym , we wish to find the
length of their longest common substring, that is, the largest k for which there are indices
i and j with xi xi+1 . . . xi+k−1 = yj yj+1 . . . yj+k−1 . Design an O(mn)-time algorithm for
this problem, and provide a complete pseudocode of your algorithm. You don’t need to
analyze your algorithm.
Q2
(10 points) Given integers n and k, along with p1 , . . . , pn ∈ [0, 1], you want to determine
the probability of obtaining exactly k heads when n biased coins are tossed independently
at random, where pi is the probability that the ith coin comes up heads. Give an O(n2 )-
time algorithm for this task. Assume you can multiply and add two numbers in [0, 1] in
O(1) time. Give a complete pseudocode of your algorithm. You don’t need to analyze
the running time of your algorithm.
Q3
(10 points) Consider the following variation of the maximum flow problem: Each vertex
also has a capacity on the maximum flow that can enter it. Show that this problem can
be solved efficiently. You don’t need to analyze your algorithm.
Q4
(10 points) Suppose someone presents you with a solution to a max-flow problem on some
network. Give a linear time algorithm to determine whether the solution does indeed
give a maximum flow. You don’t need to analyze your algorithm.
Q5
(10 points) Prove the following: For any value α, no polynomial-time α-approximation
algorithm for the traveling salesman problem exists unless P = NP. You may use the
fact that Hamiltonian Cycle is NP-complete.
Q6
(10 points) Show that the following problem (called Maximum Common Induced
Subgraph) is NP-hard: Given two graphs G1 = (V1 , E1 ) and G2 = (V2 , E2 ) and a
budget b, the goal is to compute two sets of nodes V1′ ⊆ V1 and V2′ ⊆ V2 whose deletion
leaves at least b nodes in each graph, and makes the two graphs identical.
Q7
(10 points) The Vertex Cover problem is defined as follows. Given a graph G = (V, E)
and an integer k, find a set X of k vertices such that, for any edge uv ∈ E, either u or v
is contained in X. Give any exact algorithm for this problem running in time f (k) · nO(1)
for some function f . You don’t need to analyze your algorithm.
